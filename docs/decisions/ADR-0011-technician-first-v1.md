# ADR-0011: Technician-first V1

**Status:** Accepted\
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Decision

V1 concentrates on technician operations with email, API, monitoring, and technician-created intake. The full client portal follows later.

## Consequences

Technician workflow quality, routing, collaboration, search, and operations take precedence. Client-visible replies and future visibility boundaries must still be designed correctly from the start.
