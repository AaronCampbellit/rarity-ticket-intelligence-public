# ADR-0006: Primary owner and collaborators

**Status:** Accepted\
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Decision

A work record supports multiple simultaneous assignees with at most one primary owner accountable for completion.

## Consequences

Collaborators, reviewers, escalation engineers, account participants, watchers, and AI participants have explicit roles. Assignment does not implicitly grant every permission. Ownership changes and work claiming are audited.
