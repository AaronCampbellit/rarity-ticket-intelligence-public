# ADR-0016: V1 service desk scope

**Status:** Accepted\
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Decision

V1 is technician-first and supports Incident, Service Request, Change, and Problem records; queues/triage, one active primary owner, collaborators, configurable routing/workflows/SLAs, internal notes/public replies, time/contracts/CSV billing export, internal knowledge, dashboards, and global permission-aware search.

## Consequences

The client portal, client-facing knowledge, invoicing/payments, direct accounting integration, Slack, and new record bases for alert/security/onboarding/procurement are deferred. Alerts are incidents with alert metadata; other needs use classifications/templates/workflows.
