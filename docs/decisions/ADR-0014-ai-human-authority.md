# ADR-0014: AI assists; humans retain authority

**Status:** Accepted\
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Decision

AI may summarize, classify, correlate, search, recommend, and draft, but it may not silently make material customer-data changes.

## Consequences

AI output is labeled, permission-scoped, client-isolated, explainable, auditable, and governed by explicit approval policy. Models and providers remain replaceable platform dependencies.
