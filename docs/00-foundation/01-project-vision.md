# Project Vision

**Status:** Draft for approval\
**Version:** 0.1\
**Last updated:** 2026-07-25

## Mission

Rarity provides MSPs with a secure, reliable, self-hosted operations platform that technicians trust. Ticket Intelligence is the first application, not the boundary of the platform.

## Product boundary

One installation serves one MSP and many client organizations. It runs on MSP-controlled hardware, private cloud, or public cloud. The platform must scale from a small MSP to large enterprise operations without introducing a second architecture or permission model.

## V1 audience and scope

V1 prioritizes technicians. Intake comes from email, authenticated APIs, monitoring integrations, and technician-created records. The client portal and endpoint command execution are explicitly deferred.

## Outcomes

Technicians can rapidly triage, assign, collaborate on, resolve, search, and audit work. Administrators can configure routing, workflows, permissions, integrations, SLAs, and automation without client-specific code. Operators can understand health, protect data, and prove restoration.

## Future platform

Rarity is the PSA system of record. The same foundation hosts service delivery, Opportunities, proposals, Projects, resource planning, commercial controls, assets, documentation, contracts, reporting, knowledge, automation, and AI assistance. Client portal, monitoring, remote access, procurement, invoicing, payments, and accounting synchronization may extend that PSA foundation in later releases.

## Success

Rarity succeeds when it is predictable, fast, secure, recoverable, explainable, pleasant to use, easy to integrate, and operable without vendor-controlled infrastructure.
