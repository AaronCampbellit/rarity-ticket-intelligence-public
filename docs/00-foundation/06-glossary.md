# Glossary

**MSP:** The single managed service provider operating an installation.\
**Client:** A customer organization managed by the MSP.\
**Work record:** Shared foundation for incidents, service requests, problems, changes, alerts, general tickets, and configured record types.\
**Owner:** The one technician accountable for a work record.\
**Collaborator:** An assignee actively working the record without primary accountability.\
**Watcher:** A participant receiving visibility or notifications without assignment.\
**Department:** Broad organizational ownership dimension.\
**Team:** People or skill grouping.\
**Queue:** Active work-routing destination.\
**Workflow:** Versioned state machine and policies governing a record lifecycle.\
**Automation:** Versioned trigger/condition/action process acting through supported application services.\
**Inbound event:** Normalized representation of email, API, monitoring, or other intake before it becomes or updates a record.\
**Platform event:** Versioned fact emitted after a meaningful action.\
**Dependency graph:** Directed, typed, versioned relationships among assets, services, and other relevant objects.\
**Confirmed impact:** Impact explicitly verified by a technician or trusted source.\
**Predicted impact:** Clearly labeled inference derived from graph or correlation logic.\
**Break-glass account:** Tightly controlled local emergency identity used when federated identity is unavailable.
