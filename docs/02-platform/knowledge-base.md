# Knowledge Base

**Status:** Accepted constrained V1 scope

Knowledge articles are versioned, permission-aware objects related to clients, assets, services, work, and problems. V1 is internal-only: authorized technicians can publish directly, with drafts and version history. Client-visible knowledge and portal publication are deferred.

Search and AI may recommend authorized articles. Publication, expiration, review cadence, and accepted-answer feedback are auditable.
