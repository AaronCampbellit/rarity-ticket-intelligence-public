# Queues

**Status:** Draft

Queues are active work-routing destinations such as New Tickets, Backup Alerts, VIP Support, After-Hours, or Escalations. A queue may contain individual and team membership, managers, schedules, capacity policy, SLA defaults, and overflow rules.

Until claimed, a queue is operationally responsible for work. Routing may use round robin, skills, capacity, client defaults, and overflow, but every decision must be deterministic and explainable. Returning, transferring, claiming, and escalating work are audited actions.
