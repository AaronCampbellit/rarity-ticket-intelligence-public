# Identity and Access

**Status:** Draft

Human users, service principals, integrations, automations, and AI agents have explicit identities. Authentication establishes identity; authorization evaluates actor, capability, object, client scope, relationship, and context.

Entra ID is the optional primary workforce identity provider. Every installation instead begins with at least one network-restricted local Platform Administrator and remains fully administrable without Entra. Local accounts are limited to the Platform Administrator role, monitored, and auditable; Entra-backed users receive ordinary workforce roles. Sessions, API credentials, membership, delegation, and impersonation are first-class auditable objects.
