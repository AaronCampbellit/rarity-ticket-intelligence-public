# Code of Conduct

Rarity contributors must create a professional, inclusive, and security-conscious environment.

Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, document disagreement honestly, protect confidential information, and assume good intent while still requiring evidence for material claims. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of private information, and retaliation are not acceptable.

Security concerns should be reported privately through a future documented security-reporting channel rather than exposed publicly. Until that channel exists, do not include exploit details or sensitive data in public issues.

Project maintainers may remove harmful content or participation to protect contributors and users.
